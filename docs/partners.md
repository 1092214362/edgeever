# Sponsors & Partners

EdgeEver remains open-source, transparent, and neutral, while welcoming sustainable collaborations with the broader commercial and developer ecosystems. We look forward to partnering with enterprises, cloud platforms, AI services, and developer tool teams whose mission aligns with ours.

---

## Sponsorship Will Support

* **Multi-Platform Client Evolution**: Maintaining and enhancing smooth experiences across macOS, Windows, Linux, iOS (native Swift), and Android (Expo).
* **Engineering & Release Infrastructure**: Building robust automated testing pipelines, physical device testing, code signing, and global release distribution.
* **Core Performance & Architecture**: Optimizing response latency, sync reliability, and offline availability for massive note collections.
* **Documentation & Community Guides**: Authoring comprehensive deployment guides, migration workflows, and troubleshooting documentation.
* **Ecosystem Expansion**: Supporting MCP standards, multi-model integrations, and self-hosted Docker / Cloudflare runtimes.

## Principles of Collaboration

* **No Data or Privacy Compromise**: Sponsorship is never traded for user data, private telemetry, or undisclosed product insertions.
* **Independent Roadmap**: Sponsorship does not dictate the product feature roadmap; architectural decisions remain guided by long-term community value.
* **Explicit Mutual Agreement**: Display formats, logos, and promotional scope are clearly agreed upon prior to implementation.
* **Transparent & Traceable**: The utilization of funds or infrastructure credits remains clear, justified, and traceable.
