# 🤝 Sponsors & Partners

We remain fully open-source, transparent, and neutral, while welcoming sustainable collaborations with the broader commercial and developer ecosystems. We invite enterprises, cloud providers, AI platforms, and developer tooling teams to partner with us.

---

## Why Sponsor EdgeEver

* **High-Value Technical & Knowledge Worker Audience**: EdgeEver brings together software engineers, self-hosting enthusiasts, researchers, and productivity power users who possess sustained, high-lifetime demand for cloud storage, compute platforms, developer tools, and AI services.
* **Full-Stack Cross-Platform Matrix**: Spanning macOS, Windows, Linux desktop apps, iOS (native Swift) and Android (Expo) mobile apps, alongside Docker and Cloudflare Serverless runtimes—delivering global, multi-platform brand exposure.
* **Pioneer in AI Agent & MCP Ecosystem**: Deeply integrated with the Model Context Protocol (MCP) and AI Agent workflows, positioning sponsors at the forefront of the modern AI developer wave.

## Principles of Collaboration

* **No Data or Privacy Compromise**: Sponsorship is never traded for user data, private telemetry, or undisclosed product insertions.
* **Independent Roadmap**: Sponsorship does not dictate the product feature roadmap; architectural decisions remain guided by long-term community value.
* **Explicit Mutual Agreement**: Display formats, logos, and promotional scope are clearly agreed upon prior to implementation.
* **Transparent & Traceable**: The utilization of funds or infrastructure credits remains clear, justified, and traceable.
